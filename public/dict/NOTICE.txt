Dictionaries bundled with YoomiRead

English (en.json.gz, en-more.json.gz): definitions from WordNet 3.0, Copyright 2006 by Princeton University, under
  the WordNet 3.0 license (full text in WORDNET-LICENSE.txt, which must stay with these files). Senses are shortened.
  Pronunciations from ECDICT by Linwei (skywind3000/ECDICT), MIT License. Common words are in en.json.gz, the rest
  in en-more.json.gz.

Chinese (zh.json.gz): a shortened copy of CC-CEDICT (mdbg.net), licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  Pinyin and English glosses are kept; the entries are shortened.
  Share-alike: this copy is under the same license.

Japanese (ja.json.gz, ja-more.json.gz): a shortened copy of JMdict (JMdict_e), from the Electronic Dictionaries
  Research Group. This publication has included material from the JMdict (EDICT, etc.) dictionary files in
  accordance with the licence provisions of the Electronic Dictionaries Research Group. See
  https://www.edrdg.org/ for the licence (CC BY-SA 4.0). Common words are in ja.json.gz, the rest in
  ja-more.json.gz; archaic and obscure entries are left out and definitions are shortened.
  Share-alike: this copy is under the same license.

Korean (ko.json.gz): a shortened copy of the Korean entries of English Wiktionary, as extracted by Kaikki
  (https://kaikki.org, wiktextract by Tatu Ylonen). Wiktionary text is available under CC BY-SA 4.0 (and the GFDL).
  Inflected verb and adjective forms point to their dictionary word; glosses are shortened.
  Share-alike: this copy is under the same license.
